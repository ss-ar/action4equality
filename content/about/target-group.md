The primary target group of this program is women, girls, vulnerable children and school dropout, street kids, people with disabilities, widows, youths and young women. The secondary beneficiaries include health practitioner, Police, Law enforcement units, Community leaders, and Government counterparts. It is expected that significant changes, learning and development will take place among the target group and have access to basic services like education, food security, WASH, Protection, women empowerment and a sense of belonging to the society through gender equality.
