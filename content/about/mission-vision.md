## Mission Statement

To ensure sustainable community development in livelihoods, enterprise development and income generating activities, vocational training, and provision of basic services like basic education, health, water, sanitation, hygiene promotion.

South Sudan is relatively egalitarian and lacking religious extremism. International actors interested in South Sudan recognize promoting gender equality and women’s empowerment addressing gender-based violence (GBV). A key to maintaining peace and security and helping South Sudan’s economy grow. This community driven development initiatives enabled needy community groups participate in integrated activities that has increased quality gender equity in social services like WASH, Protection (GBV/SGBV, CP), Education (ECD, ALP) and self-sustainable livelihoods.

## Vision Statement

Inspired by the resilience of South Sudanese vast and rich culture, we envision a prosperous, wealthy, developed, and poverty free country, where people live in dignity, tranquility and peace.

## Mission

![AFEF community meeting](/assets/images/img-20211104-142212-563x422.webp)

Our mission is gender equality to achieve legal and systemic change to addresses violence and discrimination against women and girls in South Sudan.

Action for Equality Foundation is trying to help create lasting solutions to youth and women problems through advocacy for change, empowering people for brighter future that is secure, just, and free from poverty and diseases.
