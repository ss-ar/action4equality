We are a development facilitator helping to leverage international assistance to South Sudan. We implement in collaboration with and sometimes on behalf of relevant government departments at county, state and national levels.

AFEF is open to capacity building opportunities offered by our development partners. We translate the knowledge to empower South Sudanese to guide their own development for the betterment of the generations to come. We promise efficiency, effectiveness, integrity, accountability and transparency exhibited by the refutable organizations we have served in the years.

We have maintained cordial partnerships by network of external stakeholders and association with government counterparts, civil society organizations, the media and the private sector in promoting and advance our work.
