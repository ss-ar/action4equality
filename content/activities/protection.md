## Safeguarding the Rights and Dignity of the Vulnerable

We provide a shield for those most at risk, ensuring women, children, and displaced families have access to safety, legal support, and psychosocial care in times of crisis.

## Protection (SGBV, CRSV and Child Protection)

![A child affected by limited health services and malnutrition](/assets/images/chil.webp)

Access to health service is limited where children suffer malnutrition.

Vulnerability of women and girls to protection related concerns especially, gender-based violence in South Sudan is widespread and alarming, and a critical cross-cutting issue mainstreamed in other program sectors (WASH, Education and Livelihood) to provide access to appropriate programming for vulnerable, disaster affected and individual households.

As part of the protection strategy; AEF established and strengthen multi-sectorial approach in its projects (Addressing GBV issues and safeguarding the future of Women and children exposed to danger). Strengthening the capacities of service providers and inclusion of all relevant sectors like health, Police, Law enforcement units, Community leaders, Government institutions to bring perpetrators to book while ensuring in-depth risk mitigation in supporting survivors. In partnership with communities and other stakeholders identify and facilitate:

1. Addressing causes and contribution factors for GBV work to support communities through the strengthening of security sector actors, community leaders and members, women’s groups.
2. Responding to GBV - work with communities, local service providers and partners to create safe and confidential services for survivors of GBV (Case Management and PSS, WGFS, CFS).
3. Sustainability - works with CSOs and build their capacity to management services in their communities.

AEF and its partners will review core standards and integrate applicable standards into the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of projects and activities (including indicators).
