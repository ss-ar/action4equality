## Strengthening the Pillars of Our Community

We invest in the potential of youth and the leadership of women through skills training and economic empowerment, fostering a more equitable and self-reliant society.

![Youth and women participating in an enterprise program](/assets/images/img-20211026-102647-1504x1128.webp)

A women's rights approach to advocacy recognizes that change must be sustainable and therefore transform the barriers of unequal power relations and structures. Strengthening the multi-media campaign and Public Speaking on women rights and gender related issues.

Women economic empowerment has been adapted through collaborations and coordination with professional services, training Women on business skills, continues support after the women start their business by providing start-up kits and risk mitigation strategy.

The emphases is to start and run an enterprise, transforming unemployed and under employed youth and their household members into business owners with an improved flow of income.
