## Strategic & Professional

Action for Equality Foundation is committed to high standards of safeguarding. To this end, our team recently attended a comprehensive training session led by UNICEF and Plan International on Child Protection in Action.

![Training participants](/assets/images/unicef-plan3.webp)

This training reinforces our dedication to creating safe, inclusive environments, particularly focusing on identifying and mitigating risks to children. We are aligning our organizational practices with international standards of prevention and response, ensuring our staff are equipped to build a stronger protective shield for the children we serve.
